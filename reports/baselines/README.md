# Stage 1 baseline results

Generated by `notebooks/03_validation_and_baselines.ipynb`. These are validation folds 2022, 2023
and 2024 (expanding window from 2016), shown as the **mean across the three folds**. The locked
2025 test year was **not** evaluated. `report_lag = 0` unless stated.

Rows marked † are not operational forecasts: the oracle reads weather after the origin. Rows
marked ‡ apply a Poisson-median transform to the GLM's predicted rate, a post-processing
diagnostic for MAE.

their recall is 0. All models **under-predict high-incidence weeks**. Bias on rows at or above q90 is −2.5 to −3.5

Peak magnitude is the main remaining error.
